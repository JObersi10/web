Frequently Asked

The questions that come up most before booking.

What's your typical turnaround time?

Most edits are delivered within 5–10 business days of the shoot, depending on scope. Rush turnaround is available — mention your deadline when you reach out.

Nope, I still live with my parents and still go to high school, sorry.

Can you edit footage I've already shot?

Absolutely! Send over the raw files and a reference or two, and I'll quote the edit-and-grade-only work separately from full production.

How does payment work?

Via bank transfer or cash on-site. Exact terms are confirmed during the discovery call before anything starts.
